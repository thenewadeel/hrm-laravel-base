# Wittness Tech — Offline Marketing Strategy & Publication Plan (Australia)

**Brand:** Wittness Tech
**Product:** Wittness Tech ERP (all-in-one business management: Accounting, HR & Payroll, Inventory, Membership)
**Target sectors:** Pharmacies & pharma distributors · SMEs & trading companies · Schools & membership organisations · Manufacturing & wholesale
**Campaign style:** Old-school / print-first — flyers, one-pagers, brochures, letterbox drops, ads in local & trade publications, direct mail, in-person events
**Market:** Australia (local-first, city & suburban rollout)

---

## 1. Why print, and why now

Australian SMBs still respond strongly to trusted, physical materials:

- **Pharmacies and schools** are conservative buyers — print builds trust that cold email cannot.
- **Local trade magazines** (Pharmacy News, Australasian Pharmacy, Manufacturers' Monthly) are read cover-to-cover by their niche.
- **Federation & association channels** (Guild branches, Principal associations, Chambers of Commerce) give direct, warm distribution to the right desks.
- A **visitable demo** (drop-in at the pharmacy counter, the front office) converts where a trial account can't.

Wittness Tech is credible offline because its modules are *already* Australia-relevant (GST-compatible reporting, PAYG-style payroll structure, multi-store inventory). The materials sell pain relief, not software.

## 2. Positioning & messaging core

**One-line pitch**
> "Wittness Tech brings your accounts, staff, stock and members into one system — no more re-keying data between them."

**The core promise:** *Stop re-entering the same numbers in three different programs.* Accounting, payroll, stock and membership should update each other automatically.

**Proof points** (all true of the product):
- Payroll posting creates accounting entries automatically
- Stock movements update financial reports in real time
- Membership fees flow straight into cash management
- Multi-store tracking with unified reporting
- Built for Australian GST compliance language and reporting
- Cloud software, accessible from office and mobile

**AUDIENCE → MESSAGE MAP**

| Audience | Their pain | Wittness Tech answer | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pharmacy owner / dispensary manager** | Expiry management, stockouts, supplier reconciliation, staff rostering/payroll | Inventory + expiry tracking, purchase vouchers, payroll & accounting in one system | Request a demo at your counter |
| **SME / trading company** | Separate invoicing, spreadsheets, payroll and stock records | One system: sales, purchases, inventory, payroll, P&L | Book a 20-minute demo |
| **School / membership org** | Fees, subscriptions, membership cards, renewals | Membership module: billing, renewals, cards, fee → cash sync | Book a web demo |
| **Manufacturer / wholesaler** | Multi-branch stock, batch/expiry, costing, purchase-to-sale trail | Multi-store inventory, batch & expiry, FIFO/weighted valuation, full audit trail | Visit to see a live system |

## 6. Launch plan — 90-day calendar (4 sectors × 1 pilot city each)

**Week 1–2 — Foundation**
- Finalise one-pager, flyer, brochure (HTML → PDF via browser print)
- Build demo environment per sector (pharmacy data, school data, manufacturing data)
- Set up phone line, demo-booking QR, trackable phone/email per material
- Update Google Business Profile & directories (Yellow Pages categories)

**Week 3–6 — Direct assault**
- Letterbox drop: 5,000 flyers across 3 target suburbs (mix: pharmacy + SME areas)
- Door-to-door: 3 days/week visiting 15 pharmacies or trading stores per day with one-pager
- Direct mail: 150 personalised letters + brochure to each sector's top local prospects (600 total)
- Local newspaper business-page insert (pilot city)

**Week 7–10 — Trade & association**
- Place trade magazine display ad + advertorial (pharmacy, then manufacturing or education)
- Association newsletter inserts (Guild branch, Chamber, principal association)
- Exhibit/present at 1–2 local expos or business breakfasts
- Launch "demo morning": invite 10 invited guests; 6-monthly repeat

**Ongoing — Referral loop**
- Every converted client gets 5 brochures to hand to peers
- Ask for a 2-line testimonial for the next advertorial
- Track source per material (different phone extensions / QR codes printed per asset)
