Business Management Software for Growing Australian Businesses

Stop re-entering the same numbers in three different programs.

Witness Tech brings your accounting, HR & payroll, inventory and membership into one system — so when one module changes, the others update automatically. No more spreadsheets, no more double data entry, no more end-of-month reconciliations by hand.

Four modules, one source of truth

Accounting

Sales & purchase vouchers, cash receipts & payments, GST-ready reporting, outstandings, bank and financial-year management.

HR & Payroll

Employees, attendance & shifts, leave, loans and advances. Payroll posting creates accounting entries automatically.

Inventory

Multi-store stock, batch & expiry tracking, reorder alerts, FIFO / weighted-average valuation — synced to your financials.

Membership

Subscriptions & renewals, member cards, fee collection — fees flow straight into cash management.
